# Fees and product terms (demo bank)

## Current accounts
- Everyday Account: no monthly fee. Arranged overdraft interest 29.9% EAR variable. Unarranged overdraft: no fee, but payments may be declined.
- Premier Account: 15 GBP per month, waived when the balance stays above 10,000 GBP. Includes travel insurance and a dedicated adviser line.

## Statements
- Paper statements: free, posted within five working days.
- Printed statement in branch: free, available immediately for the last 24 months.
- Statements for rental or visa applications: the branch can print a stamped statement on request. Three months is the usual period landlords ask for.

## Mortgages
- Mortgage appointments are with a qualified adviser and last 45 minutes.
- Bring proof of income for the last three months, photo identification and details of any existing borrowing.
- Agreement in principle usually takes one working day after the appointment.

## Contact details
- Phone numbers and email addresses can be updated in branch with identification, or in the app.
- A change of postal address needs one proof of address dated within the last three months.
