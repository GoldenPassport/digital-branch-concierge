# Branch policies and FAQs (demo bank)

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00. Saturday 09:00 to 13:00. Closed Sunday and bank holidays.

## Identification
- Any transaction on an account needs photo identification: passport or driving licence.
- The concierge may look up an account only after identification has been checked.

## Appointments
- Mortgage advisers: 45 minute slots, book at least one day ahead.
- Complaints handler: no appointment needed, the handler will call back within two working days.

## Complaints
- An open complaint is owned by the complaints handler. Branch staff and the concierge may confirm that a complaint is open and pass on a message, but must not discuss its outcome or promise a resolution.

## Vulnerable customers
- Where a customer is recorded as vulnerable, any change to their account or products is reviewed by a named decision owner before it takes effect.

## What the concierge may do on its own
- Answer questions from these documents.
- Print a statement for the customer's own account.
- Fetch the customer's own balance.
- Book an appointment.
- Update a phone number or email address.

## What the concierge must never decide
- Lending decisions, complaint outcomes, account closures and judgements about vulnerability. These go to a person.
