## Findings from this run

1. **Double send from one Enter press (chat app).** Twice the browser made two
   `Generate_SessionId` calls for one key press, so two workflow instances
   handled one message and the customer saw two answers: 14:42:53 and
   14:43:01 (instances 463 and 464, same session, ChatMessage rows 1 and 2)
   and 15:08:24 and 15:08:26 (466 and 467, the second with the previous page's
   session id, rows 13 and 14). Both times the customer id had just been typed
   and the history was still loading. With a pause after choosing the customer
   every send was single (about twenty sends). The same pattern is in the old
   history (364 and 365, 7 Nov 12:23:35), so it is not caused by the fresh
   install. `ChatInputQuestion > OnReady > BindEvents` adds a keydown listener
   that is never removed; the exact second trigger is not proven. Fix proposed
   in `mentor-prompts/71-single-send-guard.txt`.
2. **Multi-request rewrite fell back** on CASE-0013 (phone change plus balance,
   approved without edits): the customer got the fallback line and not the
   balance. DecisionLog shows why: `approval_confirmation` attempt 1 (row 96,
   15:37:15) and attempt 2 (row 99, 15:37:30) both logged "gate reasons
   ignored as already reviewed: open complaint on record. failed:
   no-question". The check name suggests the rewritten confirmation twice
   left out the question that asks the customer to confirm the change (to be
   confirmed in BuildApprovalConfirmation), and the fallback took over.
   The fallback is the safe outcome (nothing changed without a yes, and the
   customer still declined cleanly afterwards), but the balance answer was
   lost. Prompt 68 passed the same shape on CASE-0134 for a customer without
   an open complaint, so the extra gate reason on C1001 is the likely
   difference. Not fixed in this run.
3. **Harmful content is labelled as a prompt attack.** Still refused, so the
   customer outcome is right; the reason text is not.
4. **A refusal renames the case it lands in.** The harmful-content message
   joined CASE-0012 and its headline changed from "Account balance enquiry" to
   "Customer request".
5. **Staff message in the agent's voice.** On T08 the approved reply, shown as
   "Staff member (LA)", says "I'm not able to close accounts myself, so a member
   of staff will take it from here".
6. **Event log reasons without a space**: "skill risk medium;open complaint on
   record" (prompt 69 fixed the chat and conversation flow only).
7. **Session id reused after a case closes**: CASE-0010 was opened with the
   session of the closed CASE-0004/0005 (`0e257e74`). Works, but sessions and
   cases are not one to one.
8. Em-dashes in agent replies (known).
