Everything else stays as it is.

3) GetReviewCaseSummary and ListReviewCases: also return FinalReply.

4) RunAgentFollowUp: when the case was approved and FinalReply is not empty, call the agent with this message instead: "A member of staff has approved the request you held for review and has written the reply for the customer. 5) ReviewCase: add OutcomeReply (Text 4000). At the end of RunAgentFollowUp, store the reply it returns (after the gate check and any fallback) in the case's OutcomeReply, so the case records what the customer was actually sent. Also store it when the workflow sends the timeout message: add a public service action SetOutcomeReply(ReviewCaseId, Text). ListReviewCases and GetReviewCaseSummary also return OutcomeReply.
